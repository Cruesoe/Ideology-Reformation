[b]Let your colony's beliefs grow one tradition at a time.[/b]

Ideology Reformation makes Fluid ideoligion reforms smaller, clearer and more meaningful.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Make one deliberate gameplay change per reform.
[*]Add or remove a meme, change a precept or structure, or edit one ritual or role.
[*]Freely change names, colours, icons and styles.
[*]Review every change before spending development points.
[*]Go back or reset without consequences.
[*]Preserves compatible precepts when memes change.
[*]Unlock selected memes through research.
[*]Research projects show which memes they unlock.
[/list]

[b]Compatibility[/b]
[list]
[*]Alpha Memes
[*]Vanilla Ideology Expanded
[*]Vanilla Vehicles Expanded and Vanilla Genetics Expanded
[*]Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey memes
[*]Xenotype And Ideology Buttons TitleScreen: every meme can be picked in its main-menu Ideology creator
[*]Existing saves
[/list]

Only Fluid ideoligions are affected. Fixed ideoligions keep vanilla behaviour.

See the discussion for the memes affected so far. You're welcome to suggest changes and additions.
