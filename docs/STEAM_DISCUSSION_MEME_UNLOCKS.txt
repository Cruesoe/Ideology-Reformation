[h1]Ideology Reformation — Meme Unlock Guide[/h1]

Ideology Reformation makes selected memes become available as your colony advances. This reference lists every supported meme and the requirement, if any, added by the mod.

[list]
[*][b]Research requirement[/b] — complete the named research project.
[*][b]Tech-level requirement[/b] — advance the colony to at least that technology level.
[*][b]Available immediately[/b] — Ideology Reformation adds no progression requirement. The relevant DLC or content mod must still be enabled.
[/list]

[i]Current coverage: 133 memes and structures from 11 supported sources; 52 have progression requirements and 81 are available immediately.[/i]
