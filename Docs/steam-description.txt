[b]Let your colony's beliefs grow one tradition at a time.[/b]

Ideology Reformation makes Fluid ideoligion reforms smaller, clearer and more meaningful. See the discussion for the memes affected so far; you're welcome to suggest changes and additions.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Each reform makes one deliberate gameplay change.
[*]A reform can add or remove a meme, change a precept or structure, or edit one ritual or role.
[*]Names, colors, icons and styles can be changed freely.
[*]Every change can be reviewed before spending development points.
[*]You can go back or reset without consequences.
[*]Compatible precepts are kept when memes change.
[*]Selected memes are unlocked through research.
[*]Research projects show which memes they unlock.
[*]Only Fluid ideoligions are affected; Fixed ideoligions keep vanilla behavior.
[/list]

[h2]Settings[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Use highest researched tech level[/b]: uses the highest tech level among completed research instead of the colony's actual tech level. On by default, or off when Node Research is active.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works with Alpha Memes, Vanilla Ideology Expanded, Vanilla Vehicles Expanded, Vanilla Genetics Expanded, and the Biotech, Anomaly and Odyssey memes. With Xenotype And Ideology Buttons TitleScreen, every meme can be picked in its main-menu Ideology creator.
Safe to add to an existing save.
